Transparent Cart & Checkout

1

Smart Search & Discovery:

Intuitive search functionality featuring "Popular Searches" to highlight your top items and boost order conversions.

2

Dynamic Cart Management:

Customers can easily review their selections, adjust item quantities seamlessly with a tap, or remove items entirely before finalizing their purchase.

3

Accurate Cost Breakdown:

Automatically calculates and displays Subtotals, localized VAT (e.g., 5%), and dynamic Delivery Fees to ensure complete pricing transparency before the user taps "Place Order."

4

Address Selection:

Integrated delivery address management ensures accurate routing and fee calculation based on the customer's location.

Comprehensive Order History

1

Centralized Dashboard:

Users can track their entire purchasing lifecycle under "My Orders," viewing clean summaries that include order reference numbers, dates, timestamps, item counts, and total costs.

2

Real-Time Status Tracking:

Clear visual indicators (such as "Cancelled" or "Completed") keep the customer informed of their order status without needing to contact support.

3

Granular Item Breakdown:

Tapping into an order reveals the exact details of the transaction, including individual item prices, complex bundle descriptions (e.g., Meals with multiple sides and drinks), and applied fees.

4

One-Click Reordering:

A major driver for customer retention. The prominent "Reorder Items" button allows customers to duplicate their favorite past orders instantly, minimizing friction and increasing customer lifetime value.

Step 2: Open App Builder → Applications

Step 3: Create a New Application (Enter the required application details: Application Name, Website URL, Email Address, Phone Number, Country Code)

Step 4: Configure Authentication Details (Site URL, License Key (ODOO-RESTAURANT-APP), Remote Build ID (generated automatically after synchronization))

Step 5: Enter the Email Address, Phone Number, Country Code (These details are used for build notifications and application ownership information.)

Step 6: Upload Application Assets (Logo Image, App Icon, Splash Screen Image, Google Services JSON)

Step 7: Save & Sync to API
